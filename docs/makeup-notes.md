# Makeup shade notes (Glowtone Makeup tab)
Shades in `js/makeup.js` follow the same consultant criteria as `docs/korean-12-type-research.md`. Each type's makeup sticks to its undertone (golden/yellow-based shades for warm, blue/pink-based for cool), its lightness range (light types use sheer, light shades; deep types use deep shades), and its clarity (mute types use grayed/MLBB tones, bright types use clear, saturated ones).
- Examples consultants commonly give: Autumn lips in brick red, terracotta and burgundy-brown; Winter lips in red, wine and hot pink; Summer blush in cool pink; Spring lips and blush in coral, peach and apricot. [Glowmi][Beauty Insight]
- Foundation: Korean shade names pair a depth number (13/17/21/23/25…) with an undertone letter (C/P = cool or pink, N = neutral, W/Y = warm or yellow). Match the undertone letter to the result, and the depth to the person's own skin. Test on the jawline in daylight and allow time for oxidation.
- Finish tips (dewy/satin/matte) are styling suggestions: clear types tend to suit glossy, juicy finishes and mute types suit velvet or subtle finishes. They are not hard rules.
- The hex values are representative swatches, not specific products. Product picks will come later.
